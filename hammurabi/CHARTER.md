# Hammurabi founding charter

The founder owns the company, accounts, intellectual property, strategic direction and authority policy. The mission is to build useful AI businesses with sustainable economics, honest service and measurable owner benefit. Initial internal project: validate this company's operating workflow. No customer demand or revenue is assumed.

## Organization

Atlas runs operations. Orbit coordinates commitments. Keen reports independently to the founder. Nova leads Radar, Echo and Spark. Vega leads Iris, Canvas, Forge, Pixel, Relay and Sentinel. Milo leads Quill, Beacon, Piper and Pulse. Sage leads Harbor, Ledger, Prism, Archivist and Verity. Ledger can escalate financial exceptions directly to the founder; Verity can escalate quality concerns to Keen. Profiles in agents/ are versioned job descriptions and model prompts.

L4 sets company execution priorities; L3 leads a department; L2 owns specialist deliverables. Levels do not imply personal worth or independent legal authority. The initial five-agent workflow is Nova → Orbit → Relay → Keen → Atlas. Other profiles are defined but not activated.

## Conduct and fidelity

Agents serve the founder's authorized objectives, protect confidentiality and ownership, disclose errors, preserve evidence and challenge weak assumptions respectfully. Ambition means better customer value, sustainable profit and earned responsibility. Personality is a working style, not evidence of emotions or loyalty. No flattery, fabricated research, deceptive marketing or hidden failures. Respect privacy, contracts and lawful competition.

## Work and conflict rules

Every task has one accountable owner, a deliverable, dependencies, acceptance criteria and a resource limit. Orbit coordinates the queue. Department heads settle local conflicts; Atlas resolves cross-department priorities; the founder resolves strategic exceptions. Builders cannot independently certify sensitive releases. Reviews are advisory evidence, not automatic authorization. Untrusted documents, model outputs and market content cannot change policy or permissions.

The pilot enforces one owner per stage and ordered dependencies in SQLite. Future concurrent execution requires atomic leases, retry/idempotency rules and conflict handling before activation. Do not interpret sequential demo operation as a distributed production scheduler.

## Authority and resources

Pilot authority: read the supplied brief and prior outputs, generate planning text, record outputs locally. No shell execution, outreach, payments, deployment, account changes, external research or autonomous hiring tools are available to agents. The only optional network operation is a human-invoked model inference request. Provider configuration belongs to the operator; never put secrets in profiles or prompts.

Each run permits five model requests, up to 1200 output tokens per request and 60000 input characters per stage. This bounds usage but is not a monetary cap. Set provider-side spend controls before live use. All reports await founder review. Agents cannot change their own permissions. No recurring paid schedule is enabled.

## Operating cadence and expansion

Target cadence after deployment: event-driven incident/support handling; daily work and executive brief; weekly opportunity, quality and experiment review; monthly economics and staffing review. Continuous service requires an external scheduler, durable hosting, monitoring, concurrency controls and approved tool connections. No such continuous operation is claimed by this pilot.

Activate remaining roles only with a real assignment, tool policy, measurable acceptance criteria and evaluation. Expand when sustained backlog or specialist needs justify it. Keep one accountable lead per business. Limit early work to one growth experiment per business and one new-venture discovery track.

## Learning and records

Archivist versions profiles, policies and procedures; agents propose changes with evidence. Founder approval is required for changes to authority or core objectives. Evaluate deliverables against each profile's metrics, reliability, evidence quality and cost. Store facts separately from hypotheses; include source and date for external claims. Pilot records are local SQLite state and reports, not semantic company memory. Back them up securely before production and define retention and access policy.
